Example NFC Service Privacy Notice

Notice version: 2026-10-29
Operator: Example Service Operator

This notice belongs only to the repository's demonstration reader configuration. Do not use the example identity or metadata for a production service.

Requested data and purposes

The example provider allocates an opaque credential reference and requests a profile-specific public authentication key to verify approvals. The reference associates that key with the provider account selected during the provider's OIDC login. The reference is not supplied by the phone and the example does not request an alias or any other field.

Storage and transfer

Online registration and renewal use HTTPS with this example provider. During NFC use, authenticated application-layer encryption protects the phone-to-terminal hop and the terminal submits the signed approval to the bridge over HTTPS. The provider bridge stores the OIDC issuer and subject, opaque credential reference, public authentication key, profile, registration/expiration timestamps, and last verified authentication or explicit renewal time in PostgreSQL. It stores no access tokens, refresh tokens, NFC session keys, or authentication attempts. It includes no analytics or tracking.

Retention and renewal

The default registration lifetime is 548 days. A verified authentication or explicit user-approved renewal extends it by another 548 days. The phone sends no heartbeat. Expired records are ineligible for authentication or renewal and are deleted after a 30-day cleanup grace period.

Deletion

Use the provider's configured credential-management endpoint to revoke and delete the corresponding credentials row. In the demonstration stack, an operator may instead delete the row from the PostgreSQL database or remove the development volume to remove all registrations. Deleting the registration on the phone does not remove this provider-side copy.

Contact

Questions about this demonstration service can be submitted through the project issue tracker. A real service must replace this notice and contact with its own.
