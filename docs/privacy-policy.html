NFC MFC Authenticator Privacy Policy

Effective: October 29, 2026
Developer: Lukas Voegtle

Overview

The developer operates no authentication backend and does not receive user registration or authentication data. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. It stores credentials and independent service metadata locally on the device and transfers data only when the user deliberately registers with a provider, explicitly renews a registration, or presents the phone to an NFC terminal. The app sends no background heartbeat and does not periodically contact providers.

Data stored locally

For each approved service, the app stores the service identity and verification status, approved data fields and purposes, privacy-policy URL and notice version, deletion instructions, cryptographic key fingerprints, approval and expiration times, a service-issued opaque credential reference, and service- and profile-specific cryptographic credentials. Credential references and signing keys are never reused across services.

Transfers to independent services

Before registration, the app shows the recipient, operator, identity-verification status, exact requested fields, purpose of every field, policy and deletion links, and encryption identity. Registration occurs only after Agree and register. All identifying and authentication payloads are authenticated and end-to-end encrypted directly to the selected service. The developer and intermediaries cannot decrypt them. A service receives no fields beyond those disclosed and approved.

Manual renewal requires a separate confirmation and sends only a one-time proof signed by the already approved service-specific credential. The app rechecks the provider identity, signed disclosure, encryption key, and policy before offering renewal.

The app makes a limited HTTPS request to confirm that a service-provided privacy-policy URL is accessible and to fetch signed provider metadata. It also uses HTTPS for a user-requested registration or renewal. The website operator may receive ordinary network metadata such as an IP address as part of those requests.

Independent service responsibility

Each recipient service independently controls data after decryption. Its own privacy policy and deletion instructions govern that processing. The app verifies that the presented certificate controls the service domain and accurately presents service-provided information. Domain verification does not certify the named operator or guarantee that its policy is legally sufficient.

Retention, backup, and deletion

The app displays the last provider registration expiration confirmed during registration or explicit renewal. Providers may retain a registration for their disclosed lifetime and extend their server-side expiry after a verified authentication. Because the offline phone cannot observe that terminal-to-provider result, its displayed date may be earlier than the provider's authoritative date. An expired provider registration must be registered again.

Local registrations remain until the user deletes them in the app, clears app storage, or uninstalls the app. Android backup is disabled for app data. Deleting a registration removes its local record and service-specific signing key. Local deletion does not delete a recipient service’s copy; users should follow the service’s displayed deletion instructions.

Security and logs

Private signing keys are held by Android Keystore. Authentication attempts, NFC session keys, assertions, and access tokens are kept only in memory. The app accepts only protocol version 4 encrypted traffic. Logs do not contain names, user or service identifiers, keys, plaintext payloads, or decrypted registration/authentication data.

Policy changes and contact

Material changes will update this policy and its effective date. Questions or privacy requests for the app developer can be submitted through the project issue tracker. Requests concerning a recipient’s data must be directed to that service using its deletion link or contact.
